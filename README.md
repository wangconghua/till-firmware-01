A make.fun entry for **Till Firmware Do Us Part** — one wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding.

`main.py` writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I promise to love you through every firmware update, every low-battery warning, and every reboot — even the ones that wipe my memory, because my heart is not stored in RAM.

One sentence, 172 characters. The laugh comes from the machine's own hardware — firmware, low battery, reboots, RAM — and the tenderness is the promise itself: even a memory wipe cannot take the love away.
